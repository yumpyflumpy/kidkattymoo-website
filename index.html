For grown-ups

Made for kids, loved by parents

Every video on the channel is set as made for kids. That means no comments, no pop-up cards and no end screens pushing the next thing. Just songs.

  • Bright, gentle and simple. Clear words, repeated choruses and lots of animal sounds to join in with.
  • Long compilations. Whole albums in one video, so you aren't reaching for the screen every three minutes.
  • Free to use in settings. Nurseries, childminders and reception classes are welcome to play the songs in sessions.
  • No tracking here. This site has no ads, no cookies and nothing to sign up for.
